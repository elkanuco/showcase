# European Investment Bank (EIB) - Core Business and Services

## Overview

The **European Investment Bank (EIB)** is the long-term lending institution of the European Union. Its mission is to support EU policy objectives through financing, advisory services, and risk-sharing solutions that promote sustainable growth, competitiveness, innovation, and climate action. 

The EIB is part of the **EIB Group**, which consists of:

- **European Investment Bank (EIB)** – provides long-term financing for public and private sector investments.
- **European Investment Fund (EIF)** – specializes in financing solutions for Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs), innovation, venture capital, and guarantees.

---

# Core Business Areas

## 1. Long-Term Project Financing

The EIB finances strategic investment projects that contribute to economic development and European policy goals. Key sectors include:

- Transport infrastructure (railways, roads, ports, airports)
- Energy and renewable energy
- Climate and environmental projects
- Digital infrastructure and telecommunications
- Healthcare and education
- Research, development, and innovation
- Urban regeneration and housing

The Bank operates both within the European Union and in partner countries worldwide. 

---

## 2. Climate Action and Sustainability

As the EU's "Climate Bank", the EIB directs significant financing toward projects supporting:

- Renewable energy generation
- Energy efficiency improvements
- Decarbonization initiatives
- Sustainable mobility
- Climate adaptation and resilience
- Environmental protection and biodiversity

Climate action remains one of the EIB Group's strategic priorities. 

---

## 3. Innovation and Competitiveness

The EIB supports innovation-driven investments that strengthen European competitiveness, including:

- Research and development (R&D)
- Advanced manufacturing
- Digital transformation
- Emerging technologies
- Startups and scaleups

Financing helps organizations invest in innovation while managing long-term investment risks. 

---

## 4. SME and Mid-Cap Support

Through the European Investment Fund (EIF) and financial intermediaries, the EIB Group improves access to finance for:

- Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs)
- Mid-sized companies
- Startups
- High-growth innovative businesses

Support mechanisms include guarantees, venture capital investments, equity financing, and risk-sharing instruments. 

---

# Main Services

## Financing Services

The EIB offers a broad range of financial products, including:

### Loans

- Investment loans
- Framework loans
- Project finance loans
- Corporate financing

### Guarantees

- Credit enhancement solutions
- Risk-sharing facilities
- Portfolio guarantees

### Equity and Venture Capital

- Equity investments through EIF
- Venture capital support
- Growth capital financing

### Green and Sustainable Finance

- Climate-focused financing
- Sustainability-linked financing
- Green investment products



---

## Advisory Services

EIB Advisory helps public and private organizations prepare, structure, and implement investment projects.

### Project Support

- Project identification
- Feasibility assessment
- Financial structuring
- Investment planning
- Implementation support

### Market Development

- Design of financial instruments
- Investment platforms
- Blended finance structures

### Capacity Building

- Technical assistance
- Skills development
- Governance and compliance support

### Access to Funding

- Guidance on EIB financing
- Support for EU funding programs
- Investor readiness assistance

The objective is to improve project quality, accelerate financing access, and maximize investment impact. 

---

## Blending and Investment Platforms

The EIB frequently combines multiple financing sources to increase investment capacity and reduce project risk.

Typical funding combinations include:

- EIB loans
- EU grants
- National funding
- Private sector capital
- Institutional investors

This approach helps make large strategic projects financially viable.

# Summary

The European Investment Bank's core business is to provide **financing, guarantees, investment solutions, and advisory services** that enable high-impact investment projects.
Through the EIB and EIF, the EIB Group supports infrastructure, climate action, innovation, digital transformation, and SME growth across Europe and globally.
